Sforzando Social Publisher

Sforzando is an independent mobile game studio. Sforzando Social Publisher is the studio's internal tool for publishing short gameplay videos of its own games to the studio's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, and for reading the view and like counts of those videos.

The tool is not offered to the public and is used only by the studio.
