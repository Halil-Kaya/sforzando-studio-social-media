Sforzando Social Publisher — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. What the Tool is

Sforzando Social Publisher ("the Tool") is an internal tool of the independent game studio Sforzando. It publishes the studio's own gameplay videos to the studio's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts and reads their statistics. It is not offered to the public.

2. Use

The Tool may be used only by Sforzando and only with accounts the studio owns. All published content is created by and belongs to Sforzando. Music and other material in the videos is used only from royalty-free sources or the platforms' own licensed libraries.

3. Platform terms

Use of the Tool is also subject to each platform's terms: YouTube Terms of Service, TikTok Terms of Service and Instagram Terms of Use. The Tool uses only these platforms' official APIs.

4. No warranty

The Tool is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
